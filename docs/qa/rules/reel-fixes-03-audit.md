## 1. Suspected misses

1. **T-56's ring offset was never built — each builder pointed at the other.** `sandbox/_shared/rig.ts`:238 still reads `ring.position.y = 0.02`. `docs/qa/rules/reel-fixes-caves-03.md` line 14: "The ring lift to +0.04 is another builder's; your side is the *column tops*"; `docs/qa/rules/reel-fixes-shared-03.md` line 15: T-56's +0.04 is "owned by `reel-fixes-caves-03`, but do not regress its offset". Neither task lifted it, and the shared builder's own LOG check 3 quotes "ring y **0.02**". T-56's rule as written in `PHASE_0.75_TWEAKS.md`:66 ("a ground decal sits at ground + 0.04 with a polygon offset") is therefore unimplemented, and the new LESSONS Density row for T-56 does not claim it either. **Sure.**

2. **The fleeing seal's yaw snaps — a state change with no ease.** `sandbox/frozen-night/creatures.ts`, the seal block: `s.heading = Math.atan2(-vz, vx)` is *set* inside `if (d < 5)` and written straight to `s.m.rotation.y` on the same frame. The resting yaw is `1.2 + i * 2.1`, so the frame a kid crosses 5 m the body turns instantly to the flee bearing. `s.crawl` eases the pulse, the roll and the scale; nothing eases the turn. Against LESSONS §0 rule 3 and `reel-fixes-frozen-03.md` line 17 ("the waddle↔slide transition and the herd's departure all ease"). The Hearth LOG check 4's "world +x axis **0.00°** off its own velocity" is exactly the reading a snap produces, so the check does not catch it. **Likely.**

3. **No trim pass before the new tests (Working Rule 7).** `docs/qa/rules/reel-fixes-caves-03.md` line 24: "the suite is 25 tests … name where the 25 live and what the trim pass found before adding". The caves LOG check 10 lists only the six files and the new total (39), and the rocks LOG check 9 only "was 25; `rock.test.ts` adds 8". Neither section names a trim considered or rejected; no `DECISIONS.md` line covers it. **Likely.**

4. **T-45's idle band was probed in one scene, and the caves frames disagree with it.** `frozen-night-hammer-cu-03-02.png` shows the head on the snow, as claimed. But `caves-descent-shared-03-02.png` and `caves-descent-s1-03-01.png` (leftmost kid, the gold-and-blue block at roughly hip height in front of Isabella, above the near edge of her ring) read as a head carried, not resting. The shared builder's check 8 measured idle/walk/run on `frozen-night` only; the caves' stepped tier — the one ground `Kid.setGround` + the ±0.15 m `groundRel` clamp were written for — was never probed for the hammer. **Worth a look** (the camera is low and the kid is ~90 px tall, so the frame is suggestive rather than conclusive; a `shared-hammer.cjs` run on `caves-descent` settles it).

5. **Fern's lantern kept the demo's numbers where the sheet quoted canon.** `sandbox/bog-night/props.ts` keeps `makeLantern(G.witchLantern, 14, 7, 3.0, …)`; `docs/qa/rules/reel-fixes-bog-03.md` line 21 quotes `npcs.md` §2.6.5 "a placed `light.lantern` (`#FFB347`, 1.0, 4 m)" and says "keep the lantern at 1.0 / 4 m". The Bog LOG check 4 reports "base **14 cd**, range **7 m**, unchanged" — true against the brief's "light budget unchanged", but the canon number named on the sheet is neither met nor recorded as a departure. **Worth a look** (may simply be the T-07 physical-units conversion, in which case it wants one line saying so).

6. **The ice-fall pool is no longer legible as water in the frame that is meant to prove it.** `frozen-night-pool-approach-03-02.png`: the ground in front of Isabella reads as one continuous pale snow field; no waterline, no sheet, no ring of humps is distinguishable. The Hearth LOG check 9 reads it as "the sheet lies in its dish, the humps ring it, Isabella stands *on* the ice, no floating plate". The numeric basin check (check 5) is sound; the frame does not carry the claim, and the fix may have cost the pond its read. **Worth a look.**

7. **No frame anywhere shows the round's combined build.** Frame mtimes: flight 16:45–16:49, rocks 16:47–16:52, stations 17:02, bog 17:04–17:16, frozen 17:03–17:18, shared 17:22–17:29, caves 17:44–17:45. The caves report's deferral (c) names it and the one-line fix (`stairY(x, z) === null` in `props.ts`), and the brief allowed `props.ts` only where a prop "blocks the stair mouth", so the deferral is correct — but this is the frame Andrew will read as the stair fix. **Worth a look.**
