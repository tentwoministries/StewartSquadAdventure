## Checks implied

- **Never-run rule:** the stepped probe logs `{state, d}` per goblin every frame band and shows **every** state — `idle, alert, chase, windup, hit, recover` — reached for all three; quote the log. A pure Vitest check that the machine reaches `windup`/`hit` from `chase` at 60 Hz with the new `HIT_HOLD`.
- **Brake rule:** assert the brake (0.8 m) crosses the windup trigger — the distance at the frame `windup` is entered is quoted and is < the trigger, not asymptotic to it.
- **Hit-stop and shake:** the goblin hit raises **no** hit-stop (§2.5.7 has no such site); the existing `screenShake` call and the ring flash are byte-identical before and after (diff the lines and say so).
- **Pack spread (T-64):** at the frame the three first reach `windup`, pairwise goblin distances ≥ 0.7 m and bearings from the hero span ≥ 60°; in the unit test three goblins on one approach bearing give three target points ≥ 1.2 m apart. Also: their `atkT` at spawn differs, so they do not swing in unison (§2.5).
- **The club never enters the kid:** through every `hit` frame, club-head world position ≥ **0.30 m** from `bones.head` world position via `ssActive()`; the head's lowest point at the arc's end within 0.08 m of the ground.
- **Nothing pops:** arm rotation changes ≤ 0.35 rad per frame in every state; the windup ease spans ≥ 0.15 s (it is 0.35 s); the idle carries two incommensurate rates (name them).
- **Facing:** a probe reads a goblin's world forward axis against its bearing at `90° − b`, and headings stay wrapped to [−π, π] over a long chase.
- **Colour reservation:** every body hex (skin, dark, paint, cloth, iron, belt, totem wraps) tabled with hue and lightness against the four hero bases; each row passes on Δhue > 20° **or** ΔL > 15 %. Emissive ember exempt and named.
- **Geometry:** if the club head or the totem logs are displaced, `openEdges` = 0 (every edge shared by exactly two faces).
- **Emissive and lights:** the totem's ember bed gain ≤ 0.5 on any face > 0.1 m², the 0.06 m glint quad the only thing at 1.6+, and the scene's point-light count unchanged (the brazier adds none — `bosses.md` §2.6.2 "emissive-only").
- **Composition:** the totem read at S1 (28 m) as a lit brazier on a post and at S4 as a goblin's face; no totem occludes the S3 fight or a station subject.
- **Curve:** the dust puff and any strike decal use the scene's shared `uCurve`/`uCurveCenter` uniforms by reference.
- **Delivery:** five frames saved with `?t=golden` explicit, lowercase, `-04` suffix, 12 s after navigation, `ssStep(120)` then the state; `npm run check` green with the suite count stated (39 now, ≤ 3 added, trim first per Working Rule 7); `LOG.md`, `DECISIONS.md`, `LESSONS.md` appended once each, re-read first.
