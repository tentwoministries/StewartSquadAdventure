## The fixes

1. **T-65, the streamer becomes a pennant.** Remove the bar. Hang a pennant from the top of the **rear outer interplane strut on the left** (the strut at (0.1, 2.4) in plane space; the plane is built along +x, nose at +x): a chain of four quads, 0.28 m long each, tapering 0.16 → 0.06 m, `C.planeFin` red with the last quad `#A83828`, `DoubleSide`, hinged so each segment yaws and pitches from the previous one. `update(t, wind = 0)`: segment k's pitch = the hang (−80° at wind 0, easing to −5° at wind 1) plus `wind · (0.30 · sin(t · 7.3 + k · 1.1) + 0.18 · sin(t · 11.7 + k · 0.7))` and a yaw of `wind · 0.12 · sin(t · 5.1 + k)`, with a 0.04 rad sway at wind 0 on two rates so a parked plane's pennant still lives. The flight passes `wind = speed / CRUISE_SPEED` clamped to [0, 1]; the parked scenes call `update(t)` unchanged. It trails **aft** (−x). Total length about 1.1 m: a flag, not a line.
2. **T-66, the cockpit well** (`plane.ts`). Replace the single fuselage cylinder with three parts in the same profile and colours: the **nose** (x from +0.5 to the cowl), the **tail** (x from −2.1 to the tail), and between them an **open well**: a floor at 0.62 m, side walls 0.10 m thick to the rim height (1.45 m), a bulkhead at each end, the padded rim (`C.iron`) running round the opening. Ed keeps his socket at x = −1.35; the well is long enough for **his row and one row of two kids ahead of him** (kid row at x ≈ −0.45, z ±0.34) *and*, at the front, a second kid row (x ≈ +0.25, z ±0.34), so variant A seats all four ahead of Ed in two rows of two. The parked planes in the other three scenes show the same open well (a real cockpit, empty). Keep the footprint, the cowl, the wings, the struts (**mirror the two cabane struts to z = −0.45 as well**; today they stand on one side only), the wheels, the tail; the plane's outline in the WG station must be the plane Andrew liked.

   - **B · wing riders**: the four sit on the **lower wing** at its root, two each side of the fuselage (z ±0.72 and ±1.36, x = 0.55), facing forward, legs over the leading edge, a **lap strap** each (a 0.05 × 0.6 m band in `C.planeFin` red with an iron buckle block, from wing to wing over the thighs); the inner kid's inner hand holds the **cabane strut** (mirrored in fix 2) by `ikArm`, the outer kid's outer hand grips the strap and the other lies flat on the wing.
