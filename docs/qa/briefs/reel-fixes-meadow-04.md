## The design (Fable's call; the details are yours where this is silent)

**The body, 0.9 m, built along +x as now.** Hunched torso leaning forward 0.25 rad, a round belly under it; the oversized head (radius 0.19) with a **jaw**: a wedge under the face with two bone tusks rising from the lower lip (the underbite), a brow ridge in the dark skin over bone-white eyes with black pupils; **long ears** 0.20 m that trail in the run and flick at idle; the bone-tooth necklace kept. **Arms with elbows**: upper arm 0.16 m, forearm 0.15 m, a hand block 0.06 m, shoulders at 0.56 m, elbows bend; the arms hang at the sides at idle and go **back** in the sprint (the bible's silhouette). **The club is a thing it carries, not a limb**: 0.42 m total, a knotted head (icosahedron 0.08 with three bone spikes) on a rust-iron shaft, the grip wrapped in bone cord; it rests **on the right shoulder** at idle and chase, swings two-handed overhead in the windup and down to the ground in the hit. **Colour and features inside the reserved bands**: bone war-paint stripes on the face and chest (two or three bars, `#E6DCC3`), the Forest cloth loincloth `#4A3524` on a rope belt, one rust-iron ear ring, wide bare feet with three toes. **Three individuals**: different paint patterns, one ear nicked, club heads knot / stone / bone; the same rig. Silhouette test: at 14 m (station S3) the ears, the club on the shoulder and the paint must read; at 4 m (a study framing) the face must read as a face.

**The behaviour (T-64), on the same state machine.** *Idle*: a mill inside 1.5 m of home (a few slow steps, a stop), a scratch every 4–7 s (one hand to the head), and a look toward the active kid every 3–6 s; nothing pops (§0 rule 3). *Chase*: **each goblin aims at its own slot** on a ring of radius 0.9 m round the hero at bearings −45°, 0°, +45° relative to its own approach bearing, so the three fan out and arrive abreast, never in a file; the separation push grows to 0.7 m; the skid brake unchanged. *Windup 0.35 s*: the club eases up (smoothstep) to overhead, both hands on it, a small hop back of 0.15 m at the start. *Hit*: the swing is an **eased arc over 0.12 s** (`HIT_HOLD` 0.08 → 0.12 is allowed; update the test that pins it) ending with the club head **on the ground 0.5 m in front of the goblin**, a dust puff there (reuse the meadow's dust particle recipe from the Ground Pound, 6 particles); the hero's ring flash and the screen shake stay exactly as they are (Andrew: keep them). *Recover 0.65 s*: the club drags back to the shoulder, the goblin takes half a step back and shakes its head once. **The club never enters the kid**: with the brake at 0.8 m and the arc landing at 0.5 m the head stays clear; the probe measures it.

**The totems (T-58), `props.ts`.** A **war-totem** 3.2 m, the horde's language: a crooked post of two stacked logs with a kink, three bone-cord wraps, a **carved goblin face** in the post (the same ears, jaw and brow blocks, eye holes in the dark skin, two bone tusks), a hung bone-tooth trophy string and two hanging crystal shards below the face, the Forest-cloth rag banner kept, and at the top an **iron brazier bowl with embers** in place of the pale ball: the bowl `C.iron`, the ember bed a small emissive quad (`#FF6A2A`, ≤ 0.5 gain on any face larger than 0.1 m², a 0.06 m quad at 1.6+ for the glint), no point light (the bible: emissive-only; the scene's light budget stays). The faces keep looking in at the meadow. At S1 (28 m) the top must read as a lit brazier on a post; at S4 the face must read as a goblin's.
