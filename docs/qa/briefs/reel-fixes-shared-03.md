## The fixes

1. **T-41, the ring bends with the world.** `rig.ts` builds the ring on a `ShaderMaterial` whose vertex shader is `projectionMatrix * modelViewMatrix * position`; the kid's body is on `makeWorldMaterial`, which bends world-space y by `uCurve * d²` from `uCurveCenter` (`material.ts:75–76`). Give the ring's vertex shader the same bend on the world position, driven by the same `WORLD_U.uCurve` / `uCurveCenter` uniforms (share the uniform objects; do not copy values). The ribbon in `kid-isabella.ts` is a `MeshBasicMaterial` with vertex colours: give it the bend too (a `makeWorldMaterial` variant with vertex colours and additive blending if `material.ts`'s options allow it without editing that file; otherwise an `onBeforeCompile` that injects the same two lines). Check `material.ts` for what its shader chunk actually does before writing yours (Rule 3).
2. **T-42, the staff in Collette's hand.** Keep the staff on the root (LESSONS Rigs row 2) but *solve* it each frame: at idle the shaft stands planted 0.20 m ahead of the right foot, tilted so it passes through the right hand at chest height, and both hands are posed onto it (the hand centre is `hand` node + (0, −0.055·wf, 0) in hand space); at walk the shaft stays upright through the right hand and its foot lifts 0.10 m clear of the ground; the right arm no longer swings with the walk (the left keeps its swing). The flourish twirl (the baton twirl about the hands' height) must still read as it did: pivot at the hands. Blend idle ↔ walk on the rig's own `blend`.
3. **T-45, the hammer on the ground, not under it.** Isabella's hammer is parented to the right hand with a 0.92 m shaft below a 0.50 m hand. Re-solve it so: at **idle** the head's lowest point rests on the ground at her right (within [ground − 0.02, ground + 0.05]) with both hands on the shaft top (the bible's pose); at **walk** (`blend` → 1, speed ≤ 2.0) the head drags behind her skimming the ground (lowest point within [ground − 0.02, ground + 0.12], the shaft solved from the right hand to the head); at **run** (speed > 2.0 — read `walk.moving` is not enough; the rig gets `walking` only, so expose the speed class through `KidCtx` or a setter on the kid and have `scene.ts` set it from `walk`) the hammer comes up onto her right shoulder (head above 1.0 m, shaft across the shoulder). The dust trail (0.4 m, a puff per step) via `_shared/particles.ts` `burst` if it costs under an hour; otherwise defer and say so. The whirl (the flourish) keeps its arms-out hammer-out pose and the ribbon.
4. **T-44, the sprint lock.** In `_shared/walk.ts`: two shift keydowns within 0.3 s (ignore `e.repeat`) set `sprint = true`; the top speed is then `4.5 × 1.2 = 5.4`; holding shift alone stays 4.5; the lock clears when there has been no movement input for 0.4 s or on the next single shift tap. Same in `sandbox/forest-dusk/walk.ts` (its own copy). The HUD's keys line in `scene.ts` and the saved-frame hints (`drawOverlay`'s `hints`) read `⇧ run · ⇧⇧ sprint`; the hub's `<p class="keys">` says the same. Every ease stays (Tier-0 rule 3: the speed change eases at the existing 8/s).
