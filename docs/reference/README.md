# Visual references

Filed by Andrew on 2026-09-07: 21 frames from a small-globe low-poly game, each as `.jpg` (viewing copy, about 200 KB) and `.png` (lossless master, about 1.3 MB). Docs and the sandbox cite the `.jpg`; the `.png` is the archival copy. **These are references, not the target:** we are not building the small-globe format (Brief §5.1: floating islands with a curved-world shader), the camera may not always be third person, and the characters are ours (`docs/design/heroes.md`). What they contribute is the *look*: clean facets with flat shading, saturated jewel colours on a navy sky, chunky clouds, dense small props, a quiet serif title card, and above all the tempo of everything that moves (`MOTION_TEMPO_NOTES.md`). Andrew's brief for the studies: "do it better."

| File | Biome | What it contributes |
|---|---|---|
| `Fernwood1–3` | Forest | The north star (Brief §4.1 described these). Checkered teal roof, turquoise stream, deer and fox, the camp of tent + fire ring + logs + fruit basket, clouds below the canopy line, the `FOREST · 01 / Fernwood` card. `Fernwood3` is the closest to our C1 camp framing. |
| `CloverFields1–2` | Farm | Windmill tempo, stepping-stone path, garden beds, scarecrow, sheep, the cabin among pines. The brightest, most saturated ground of the set. |
| `Shell Cove1–3` | Beach | Palms, water edge foam, umbrellas: a second palette (sand + turquoise) to check the UI accent swap against. |
| `Sunstone Oasis1–3` | Desert | Oasis water, cacti, dunes; `3_pre-action / during-action / action-ending-camera-rotated` show a scripted interaction beat with the camera rotating: the closest thing in the set to a cutscene shot. |
| `Ember Heights1–5` | Volcano | Lava ribbons, the seismograph beat, `4-Character-Camera-Closeup` is the only close portrait of the character (backpack, hat, face: the scale of detail a close camera needs), `5-World-Zoomed-Out` is the whole-globe read. |
| `NorthLight1–3` | Arctic | Snow facets, ice crystals, polar bear and seals, the tent camp on snow: the Frozen Peaks palette check. |
