v27 has seventeen unnamed enemy types drawn as coloured ellipses with one or two glyphs, driven by a single proximity AI. They become seventeen named creatures on five shared rigs and four bespoke guardians, each readable by silhouette, motion and colour at one-eighth screen height, with every attack telegraphed on the ground, every kill dying in one of v27's four verbs, and the horde's camps and cages rebuilt as lit diorama set pieces. The numbers are v27's numbers.

## 1. What v27 does

**Roster and data (SI §11, L2733–2744).** `ETYPES` holds seventeen keys — `goblin, orc, troll, archer, bat, shielded, slime, skeleton, mushroom, fire_elemental, lava_slime, ember_sprite, obsidian_guard, wraith, brute, healer, bomber` — each with `hp, spd (px/s), dmg, sz (radius px = hit radius), col/dk, rng (px), cd (s), xp, deathStyle` and optional flags (`ranged, flying, shieldHp, split, armor, blink, charge, heals, explode`). No type has a display name (FAMILY_CANON §13.5). The full table is reproduced in §2.5 below with metres alongside. Construction order: base → Hard difficulty (hp ×1.5, dmg ×1.25, spd ×1.25) → NG+ (`NG_SCALE`, SI §26.1) → elite roll (SI §12); spawner-spawned enemies then get a wave scale (SI §14.1).

**Drawing (legacy L2807–2848).** Every enemy is a `dk` ellipse under a `col` ellipse of radius `sz`, two gold dot eyes (red for bats), a 2 px bob at 6 rad/s, and a type glyph: a brown bow arc for `ranged`, two animated wing triangles for `flying`, a blue arc at `sz+6` while `shieldHp>0`, a green puddle under the slime, white rib lines on the skeleton, a pink cap with three white spots on the mushroom, a flickering `#D580FF` ring on the wraith, horns and an orange ring while the brute charges, a white cross and pulsing green ring on the healer, a sparking fuse on the bomber. Elites add a pulsing ring at `sz+5` and a 7 px name. A red HP bar appears only when hurt.

**AI (SI §13).** One `update` for all types, in order: dying → timers → poison DoT → stun gate (a stunned enemy does nothing) → special behaviour → target the nearest unlocked, living, non-downed hero (no threat table, no leash, no perception limit: every enemy in the world converges on the party) → speed modifiers (night ×1.15, rain/storm ×0.9, blood moon ×1.25 with dmg ×1.5, slow ×0.4) → melee or ranged movement → berserk check → separation (push at 100 px/s when closer than `sz+sz+4`) → integrate. Melee needs no facing or line of sight: inside `rng` with `atkT ≤ 0` the hero takes `dmg`. Ranged kites: backs off under 120 px, closes above `rng−20`, fires a 200 px/s, 2 s, size-3 `#D84830` projectile when inside `rng`. Special behaviours: wraith blink (every 3–5 s, first 3–6 s, to 30 px behind the nearest hero within 300 px, no warning), brute charge (`flashT 0.2` as the only tell, then 0.4 s at 4× speed in a fixed direction, damage still via the ordinary contact check), healer (one ally under 70 % within 120 px healed 20 % of its max every 2.5–4 s), bomber (on death, 60 px radius, 1.5× dmg, instant), splitting (two children at half HP, half dmg, `sz−3`, one generation), shield pools (`shieldHp` 40 on the type; elite `_shield` 50 % max HP; both absorbed before HP), armour (`obsidian_guard` 30 %, minimum 1), Hunter's Mark (×1.5 damage taken), Arcane Link (Collette's hits heal her partner 25 %).

**Elites (SI §12).** Six modifiers rolled once at construction: Swift (spd ×1.4), Armored (hp ×2, spd ×0.8), Vampiric (heals 30 % of melee damage dealt), Splitting, Shielded (`_shield` = 50 % max HP; `Shield broken!` when it drops), Berserker (below 30 % HP, ×2 damage). Chance: `min(0.5, (base + min(0.15, wave·0.008)) · diffMod)` with base `0.12 + 0.04·ng` in NG+, else `0.03` from team level 5, else 0; diffMod 0.5 / 1 / 1.4 by difficulty. Elites die `glow_burst`, pay double gold, add a 40 % tier-2 gear roll, and count toward `Elite Slayer` (25).

**Death (ATMOSPHERE §9.3, SI §11.2, legacy L2763).** Four verbs — shatter (8 shards, gravity 150), dissolve (bottom-up clip, rising motes), collapse (vertical squash then 10 low shards), glow_burst (scale ×3, flash white, 12 motes on a ring, no gravity). `deathA` advances at `dt·4` for `Enemy` (L2763; 0.25 s) and `dt·1.5` for `MiniBoss` (0.67 s) — ATMOSPHERE §9.3 quotes the mini-boss rate for both.

**Spawners and cages (SI §14).** Six red-tent camps (130 HP) at fixed world fractions; first spawn at 2 s, then every 3 s, up to 6 alive (`cnt`), reduced by 3 and re-armed on every overworld wave tick (`waveTimer` 45 s → 15 s floor, legacy L9251). Biome pools with 50 / 25 / 17.5 / 7.5 % slot weights: forest `goblin, orc, troll`; desert `goblin, archer, orc, brute`; swamp `goblin, bat, troll, healer`; frozen `goblin, shielded, archer, bomber`; cave `goblin, bat, shielded, wraith`. Wave scale `(1 + w·0.04)(1 + log2(max(1,w))·0.03) · diff · adaptRatio` (2.03× at wave 20 Normal). Destroying one: 25 XP, 5 gold, `boom`, `Camp Crusher`, quest `kill_spawners`, bounty `camps`. Three cages (32×28 barred boxes with a hero-coloured dot and `RESCUE!`) at fixed fractions hold heroes 1–3; any hero within 40 px opens one: `<name> rescued!`, `All siblings found! Destroy the camps!` at three, `Squad Assembled`.

**Mini-bosses (SI §15, FAMILY_CANON §6.5).** Four named guardians placed near the non-forest camps, dormant until a hero is within 500 px, then `<name> APPEARS!`, `miniboss` growl, shake 10/0.5, and a non-blocking 2.5 s popup card (SI Part 2 §14.3; ATMOSPHERE §13.1 — deliberately not letterboxed). Crystal Golem (500 HP, reflects arrows and magic, 100 px slam for 1.5× every 4 s), Sandworm (450 HP, burrows 2.5 s every 6–8 s, immune underground, `The ground trembles...`, emerges for `dmg` in 100 px), Swamp Hydra (550 HP, splits at 50 % into two 30 % heads, `The Hydra splits!`), Frost Wyrm (480 HP, five-projectile frost cone every 5 s, 0.6× dmg, slow 2 s). Not NG+-scaled. Death: `Mini-Boss Slayer`, 60 XP, guaranteed tier-2 gear, spin-and-shrink.

**Pools elsewhere.** Dungeon waves (SI Part 2 §11.8): forest `goblin, orc, bat, healer`; cave `bat, shielded, goblin, wraith`; desert `archer, orc, goblin, brute`; swamp `troll, bat, goblin, healer`; volcanic `fire_elemental, lava_slime, ember_sprite, obsidian_guard`; frozen and default `shielded, archer, goblin, bomber`; Citadel floors 1–3 as listed in §2.5. Endless (SI §26.3): base `goblin, orc, bat, slime, skeleton, mushroom`; +`shielded, archer, troll` at wave 10; +`fire_elemental, obsidian_guard` at wave 20. Boss adds (SI §18.4, §19, §20, §21.3) are recoloured `Enemy` instances — listed for `bosses.md` in §5. Event variants: `caravan_goblin` ×8 and `treasure_goblin` (SI Part 2 §9.2).

**Loot (SI §24).** 40 % potion; 3 % tier-1 gear (troll 8 %); elites: the 15 % path is dead (`this.elite` vs `_elite`, SI §29) so they get 3 % plus the 40 % tier-2 roll; mini-boss 100 % tier-2. Gold `floor(rnd(1,3)·goldMul)`, doubled for elites; the `miniBoss`/`boss` gold branches never fire (SI §29).

**Oddities that change this design (SI §29 and my reading).** (1) `fire_elemental` has `rng 35` but is `ranged`: it retreats under 120 px and only fires under 35 px, so it hovers at ~120 px and never attacks. (2) Bomber explosions are instant on death. (3) The brute's "charge" is a 0.4 s lunge of 80 px with a 0.2 s flash as its only tell. (4) No enemy has a perception radius or leash; every enemy alive walks to the party forever. (5) Skeleton and mushroom exist only in the Endless pool; they never spawn in the campaign. (6) `flying` is rendering only. (7) Melee hits need no facing, so nothing an enemy does can be dodged. (8) Elite ring colours and several base colours sit in hero hue bands (orc purple, bat purple, slime and healer green, mushroom pink, archer orange).

### 2.2 Rules that hold for every enemy

**Scale.** All metres in this file are v27 pixels × **0.025 m/px** (40 px = 1 m), the world scale fixed in `heroes.md` §2.5.1: the 3200 px overworld is an 80 m island and the heroes run at 4.0–4.5 m/s. Hero facts this file builds on (heroes.md §2.3.1, §2.5.1, §2.5.4): heights Liam 1.52 / Noah 1.40 / Collette 1.30 / Isabella 1.14 m; capsule colliders 0.40 × 1.50 / 0.32 × 1.40 / 0.32 × 1.30 / 0.34 × 1.14 m; run speeds 4.5 / 4.375 / 4.125 / 4.0 m/s, walk 40 % of run; the hostile-projectile hero hitbox is a fixed 0.375 m for all four; Liam's melee reaches 1.8 m in a ±60° cone, Isabella's whirl 1.9 m at 360°, Noah's arrows fly at 12 m/s with a 0.06 m radius, Collette's bolts at 5.5 m/s homing at 3 rad/s with a 0.5 s slow. An enemy's hit volume is a capsule of radius `sz × 0.025` and the roster's visual height, tested in the ground plane (see Flight). Every data table in §2.5 carries the px value beside the metre value. **One deliberate departure from strict conversion, logged in §6: melee reach has a floor of 1.0 m.** Strict `rng` puts the fodder's cones at 0.5–0.85 m, smaller than a hero's own footprint, which does not read as a shape you can leave at the orbit camera (`heroes.md` made the same call for Shield Bash at 0.75 m). So the in-range check, the drawn cone or circle and the hit test all use `max(rng, 1.0 m)`; `rng` in §2.5 stays v27's number, cooldowns are untouched, and the damage rhythm is v27's. Heroes still outrange every enemy (1.8–1.9 m against 1.0–1.125 m) and the 2 m dodge clears any melee shape. Fliers are exempt: their dive and touch-burn are design values (§2.4).

**Colour reservation.** The four hero bases are final (heroes.md §2.0, §2.1): Liam sapphire `#2A62CF` (hue 220°, lightness 49 %), Noah fox orange `#EE7F24` (27°, 54 %), Collette amethyst `#9D4FD8` (274°, 58 %), Isabella ruby `#D6294E` (347°, 50 %); glows `#4A9ED8` / `#FFC46B` / `#E08CF0` / `#FFD966`. The standing rule is heroes.md §2.1.3: **no enemy body colour (base or dark) may sit within 20° of hue *and* 15 % of lightness of any hero base.** That reserves the bands 200–240°, 7–47°, 254–294° and 327–7° at hero lightness; a colour may share a hero's hue only if it is at least 15 % lighter or darker. Enemies use the bands and values heroes do not: rust skin (`#7E3320`) and rust iron (`#8B3A1E`, fittings and hide), bone (`#E6DCC3`), ash (`#6B6B6B` neutral; `#4A4750` Shadow ash), deep crimson (`#7A1F2B`: ruby's hue, 20 % darker), teal (`#2F7F7A`, `#1F6F6B`), sickly olive (`#8FA33A`, `#5C6B2E`), slate (`#4C6C78`, `#5A7078`: turned 24° off sapphire toward teal), black-glass (`#1C1A22`), snow-plate (`#D8E2EA`), and **void violet only inside the Shadow Realm** (a rig-level material variant, §2.9). **Silhouette first.** Each type has one add-on that survives a silhouette test (black shape on white at 1/8 screen height): quiver spike, lantern-staff, bomb overhead, rib gap, tusks, knuckle-walk, tower shield, horns, square shoulders, wingspan, spark tail, dome, cracked dome, cap, tall shroud, column. Rigs are sized so that fodder is 0.9–1.3 m, bruisers 1.7–2.3 m, guardians 2.4–3.2 m: the three tiers read as three sizes before anything else does.

**Motion is identity (ATMOSPHERE §19, lesson 3).** Every type has a movement signature in the roster and every idle carries at least two independent motion sources at incommensurate rates (breath at 1.5 rad/s like the heroes, plus a prop sway, a blink, a flare, or a hop cycle at 0.9–2.7 rad/s); elites and guardians carry three. A kid should be able to name the enemy from a moving black silhouette.

**Telegraph language (Brief §5.4, colourblind-safe).** Every attack that can hurt a hero draws a ground decal for its windup. Shape is the meaning: **cone** = a melee swing from where the enemy stands; **circle** = a slam or burst centred on the enemy (or, hatched, on you); **line** = a charge, dive or projectile path; **dashed ring** = something arriving or a beneficial zone (heals). Fill sweeps from the origin outward over the windup so timing reads as motion; the last 0.1 s flashes white. Colours: hostile fill `tele.hostile` `#E0452E` at 35 %, outline `tele.outline` `#FFFFFF` at 90 %, 2 px at 1080p; beneficial (heals) `tele.benefit` `#3AF0FF` dashed; elite and guardian area attacks add `tele.hatch` diagonal stripes so "big and dangerous" is a pattern, not a hue. A hero is hit only if inside the shape when the windup ends; melee therefore becomes dodgeable (v27 melee could not be). Windup lives inside the v27 cooldown: the attack starts when `atkT ≤ 0` and in range, lands at `+windup`, and `atkT` resets to `cd` from the start, so the damage rhythm is v27's.

**Perception and leash (new).** An enemy notices a hero within **14 m** (560 px; a design value, kept); until then it idles or patrols its camp or nest. It targets the nearest unlocked, living, non-downed hero (v27 rule) while any hero is within 14 m, and returns to its spawn point at walk speed once no hero is within 14 m and it is more than **40 m** from home. Boss adds and event enemies have no home and never leash. Night, weather and blood moon multipliers port unchanged (§2.5). Separation ports: push apart at 2.5 m/s (100 px/s) when closer than `r₁ + r₂ + 0.1 m` (4 px).

**Flight (new, for the two fliers).** Hit tests are in the ground plane: a hero attack connects when the enemy's hit disc overlaps the attack shape in XZ, whatever the enemy's altitude, because `heroes.md` defines no vertical hit volume and v27 had none. Altitude is presentation, so the picture must agree with the rule: a flier's body flies inside the band every basic attack visibly sweeps. The **Bat flies at 0.9 m** (body centre, bob ±0.1 m) and the **Ember Sprite at 0.9 ± 0.3 m** (0.6–1.2 m), which puts both inside Isabella's hammer ring (hip height on a 1.14 m kid, about 0.5–0.8 m), under Liam's 1.52 m sword arc, at the release height of Noah's arrows and on the path of Collette's homing bolt. Their hit capsules run from 0.4 m to 1.4 m above the ground at radius 0.20 m, so any future 3D test would also connect. Fliers pass over ground props up to 0.5 m tall (stumps, low rocks, fence rails) but not walls or trees. Face height is scarier than head height; a bat at your eyes is the right read.

**Faction language.** The **horde** (Goblin, Goblin Archer, Goblin Healer, Goblin Bomber, Orc, Troll, Shieldbearer, Brute) shares rust skin or rust fittings, bone trinkets and crude iron; its camps are rust hide and bone. The **wild** (Bat, Slime, Sneaky Shroom, Skeleton, Wraith) comes from nests and belongs to the island. The **burnt** (Magma Slime, Ember Sprite, Cinder Elemental, Obsidian Guard) is ash and ember and belongs to the Shadow Realm. Goblins on each island wear a scrap in the island's own cloth so the same rig reads local: Forest rot-brown `#4A3524`, Desert bone `#EDE3CF`, Bog teal `#2F7F7A`, Frozen snow fur `#F2F7FF`, Shadow Realm ash `#605C66` (a vertex-colour slot, no new asset).

### 2.4 Behaviour in 3D

**Shared state machine** (every non-guardian enemy):

```
dormant ──(hero < wakeRange)──▶ wake (per type, 0.3–1.2 s) ──▶ idle
idle ──(hero < 14 m)──▶ alert (0.3 s: head turn, alert cue) ──▶ chase
chase ──(in range, atkT ≤ 0)──▶ windup (telegraph draws) ──▶ hit (1 tick) ──▶ recover (cd − windup) ──▶ chase
chase ──(no hero < 14 m and > 40 m from home)──▶ return ──▶ idle
any ──(stunT > 0)──▶ stunned (v27: does nothing at all; 3 warm-white motes orbit the head) ──▶ previous
any ──(rootT > 0)──▶ rooted (velocity 0, steering off; windup, hit and recover continue; a vine decal 0.6 m at the feet) ──▶ previous
any ──(hp ≤ 0)──▶ dying (death style, §2.5) ──▶ removed
```

Ranged types replace `chase` with `kite`: back off at full speed under **3.0 m** (120 px), close above `rng − 0.5 m` (20 px), damp velocity ×0.95 inside the band, and fire from anywhere inside `rng`. Hostile projectiles: **5.0 m/s** (200 px/s), life 2 s, damage = the type's `dmg`, hit the hero within 0.375 m + 0.075 m (the heroes.md §2.5.1 hitbox plus the dart's radius; v27 15 px + 3 px), halved and deflected by Liam's Shield Front from his front ±45° (heroes.md §6; the projectile carries that rule, not the enemy), blocked by obstacles and walls (new: cover matters in 3D; the projectile dies on the obstacle with 6 sparks), drawn as an emissive `#D84830` dart with a 0.4 s trail and, at night, a small bloom halo — never a point light.

### 2.8 Camps, nests and cages

**Goblin camp** (v27 `Spawner`; the numbers port). A 5 m ring on cleared ground: a central **war-totem** — a 2.4 m crooked pole of rust-lashed timber topped with a bone skull and two pennants, a brazier bowl at its foot — two or three **hide tents** (1.6 m tall, rust hide `#8B3A1E` with bone stitching `#E6DCC3`, a dark flap), a quarter-ring of sharpened stakes, and a loot pile of crates and bones. The totem is the destructible part: **130 HP**, hit by melee within `h.rng + 0.75 m` and by projectiles at 0.75 m + the projectile radius (v27 30 px); it has the only HP bar. Enemies **walk out of the tents**: first at 2 s, then every 3 s, `maxE` 6 alive (`cnt`), each tent flap lifting as its goblin ducks out. Wave tick (v27 L9251: 45 s falling to a 15 s floor, ×1.3 Easy, ×0.8 Hard): every live camp drops `cnt` by 3 and spawns at once; `Wave N` / `Wave N — Stay sharp!` announce every fifth wave from 5 (canon strings, `ui-ux.md`). Wave scale applies to camp spawns (§1). Camp residents patrol inside 6 m of the totem until a hero enters 14 m.

Camp light: the brazier is a **rust-ember flame** (`#FF5A2A`, one pooled point light intensity 2.0, range 7 m) whose flicker is one oscillator at 8.8–10 Hz driving both the flame mesh scale and the light intensity ±12 % (ATMOSPHERE §19 lesson 1: light and fire agree). It reads as *not ours* against the family camp's honey-warm fire because it is redder and its smoke column is black `#1A1616`, 3 m, drifting. Six camps per world: Forest 2, Frozen 2 (both islands have a `kill_spawners / 2` quest), Desert 1, Bog 1; the Shadow Realm has none (it has plinths and vents).

**Breaking a camp:** at 0 HP the totem cracks (0.3 s), topples over 0.8 s with a `boom` (0.35) and shake 6 / 0.2, the brazier bursts (12 ember shards, light dies over 0.5 s), the tents deflate 1.2 s later, the pennants fall, and the site becomes a **cleared camp**: charred ring, fallen pole, no spawns — a landmark the player made. Rewards as in §2.5; the remaining residents lose their home and fight to the end (no leash).

**Nests** (new; the spawn source for the wild and the burnt, so that eight types have a campaign home v27 never gave them). A nest is a prop cluster the world scatter already places, promoted to a spawner: a **Slime Puddle** (a 1.5 m wet patch by the stream), a **Bat Roost** (a dead tree with a hollow; dormant by day), a **Shroom Ring** (a fairy ring of Bog mushrooms in which two or three are Sneaky Shrooms), a **Bone Pile** (skeletons rise out of it), a **Wisp Hollow** (a hollow stump exhaling a wisp; a Wraith condenses from it), and in the Shadow Realm an **Ember Vent**, a **Magma Pool** and an **Obsidian Plinth**. A nest spawns its pool once when a hero is within 12 m and re-arms at the next dawn (night nests: at the next dusk). Nests have no HP and no reward beyond their residents; they are how the island stays alive without turning every walk into a camp assault. Counts per island are `world-builder` seeding within landmark clearance (SI Part 2 §3.1): Forest 4 puddles, 3 roosts, 2 shroom rings at night; Desert 5 bone piles; Bog 4 shroom rings, 3 roosts, 3 hollows; Shadow Realm 4 vents, 3 pools, 4 plinths, 3 ash drifts.

**Sibling cages** (v27 `Cage`; open rule ports). Three cages hold heroes 1–3 at the start of the campaign. Each is an **iron-and-timber goblin cage** 1.4 m tall and 1.2 m wide, hanging 0.4 m off the ground from a crooked gibbet pole so that it **sways** (one motion source at 0.7 rad/s, ±3°, plus the sibling's idle inside). ## 3. What preserves the magic

**ATMOSPHERE §9.3, the four death verbs — kept.** Break, melt, crumple, ascend survive as pre-fractured shards, a rising clip plane with an emissive edge, a squash with a dust ring, and a scale-and-whiten with twelve ringed motes. `grav 150` on shards versus `grav 0` on glow motes ports as 3.75 m/s² versus none, which is still the whole difference between debris and spirit. A mixed wave still dies in four rhythms; it now also dies at four heights.

**§9.2 hit sparks and the hit flash — kept.** Six sparks in the enemy's base colour at 1.5–3.75 m/s, life 0.3 s, and a 0.1 s emissive-white flash on the mesh (v27 `flashT 0.1` with `'lighter'`). The flash is what makes a hit feel like a hit; the sparks carry the enemy's colour into the air for a frame.

**§14 damage numbers — kept through `ui-ux.md`.** Enemy-dealt damage stays `#D84830`, healing `#3DCC7A`, shield absorb ice-blue, reflect `REFLECT!`, mini-boss immunity `MISS!`, with the size curve, arc and bounce. Three channels — hue, size, motion — still say who, how much, and whether it crit.

**§13.1 the mini-boss popup that does not stop the game — kept, and given a beat.** v27's decision to letterbox bosses but only card mini-bosses is the tier system in one UI choice. The 3D wake adds 1.2 s of animation, one ring, one light and a 15 % camera ease, none of which pause anything; the growl, the shake and the canon name card land exactly where they did.

**§15.2 stun stars, slow ring, mark ring — translated.** Three orbiting motes above the head, an ice rim, a ring decal in Noah's glow `#FFC46B` (the marking hero's glow token, heroes.md §2.5.12). Status is read at the enemy, not in a menu.

**§2.4 warm on cool at night — translated to enemies.** At night the enemy-side lights are the points that punch through the cool wash: rust-ember braziers (theirs), gold cage lanterns (ours, waiting to be freed), the Healer's amber lantern, the Wraith's cyan eyes, the fire family's ember cores on bloom. None is a flat glow; each is either a pooled light or an emissive on the bloom layer, so it lights or reads as lit.

**§19 lesson 1, light and fire agree — kept.** The camp brazier, the cage lantern, the Bomber's fuse and the Cinder Elemental's core each use one oscillator to drive both the emissive geometry and (where there is one) the light. When the Bomber's fuse sparks, the ground under it flickers.

**§19 lesson 2, many layers — enemies join the layering.** The fire family emits embers (Shadow), Wraiths exhale wisps (Bog), camps smoke, nests bubble or flutter, Sneaky Shrooms hide among real mushrooms. Enemies are part of the island's motion signature, not sprites on top of it.

**§19 lesson 3, incommensurate motion sources — kept as a rule.** Every idle has two rates, every elite and guardian three (the Hydra's three heads, the Wyrm's wings plus breath plus tail, the Golem's crystal pulse plus stomp). A guardian that stands still is still alive.

**§5 direction signatures — respected.** Enemy particles follow their own direction (embers rise, spores drift, sand streams from a burrowing worm) and never fight the island's ambient direction.

**§10 shake profiles — kept verbatim.** Brute stop 3 / 0.1, Bomber 4 / 0.1, camp fall 6 / 0.2, cage open 6 / 0.2, guardian wake 10 / 0.5, Golem slam 8 / 0.3, Sandworm emerge 8 / 0.3, guardian death 10 / 0.4.

**Canon threads that survive.** Every enemy-facing string: `<name> APPEARS!`, `<name> DEFEATED!`, `The ground trembles...`, `The Hydra splits!`, `Shield broken!`, `<name> rescued!`, `All siblings found! Destroy the camps!`, `Wave N` / `Wave N — Stay sharp!`, `💰 GOBLIN CARAVAN! Kill them for loot!`, `💰 TREASURE GOBLIN! Catch it!` (both with their canon emoji), `The Treasure Goblin escaped!`, the four guardian names and subtitles, and every achievement, bounty and quest name in §2.10.
