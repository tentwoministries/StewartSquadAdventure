## 4. What good looks like, in the reference's terms (`STUDY_NOTES.md` §1)

One clean key light; saturated ground under a navy sky; flat colour, one value per face, variation from props not shading; density from about twenty hand-placed things per frame, scatter sparse and clustered; a quiet card; and above all tempo: nothing pops, every recurring motion runs on two incommensurate rates and eases in and out. The anti-palette never ships (uniform mid-green, grey fog on grey ground, pastel everything, pure-black shadows, default lighting, neon UI over a soft world). When a frame argues with a bible number, keep the bible's number in the file, use the one that reads, and log the difference as a row; that is how T-07, T-16, T-17 and T-19 happened.

## 5. How this is judged afterwards

The next Fable session reads `OPUS_EXPERIMENT_NOTES.md` and the frames, runs `art-director` (Fable, xhigh) over one frame per scene with the Brief §8 rubric, and the family looks at the reel with the experiment's scenes added. Scenes that read are cherry-picked into `phase-0.75-visual-studies` one commit each; the rest stay on the experiment branch as a record. The comparison the family cares about is the open briefs (3.1–3.3): the spelled-out ones (3.4–3.5) test faithfulness, and their frames are compared against this brief line by line.
